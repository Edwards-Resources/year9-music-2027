This file used to hold the contract for **The Plot**, the world the site was built in from 16 August 2026 (the year as the room drawn from above, the stage plot and input list a band hands the sound engineer). The build no longer emits it. The contract as written is in this file's history (any commit up to `47576ad`).

## What carried over

The look did not, but three ideas did:

1. **The drawings are real documents.** Each unit is drawn as the paperwork its musicians actually use, and Term 2's blueprint sheets are their own pages. See `DESIGN.md`.
2. **Position is never carried by colour alone.** The shell holds this for every site: the current lesson says "Up to here" in words, and every section carries an icon and a word.
3. **No school identity.** The reference is production paperwork, never the school.
