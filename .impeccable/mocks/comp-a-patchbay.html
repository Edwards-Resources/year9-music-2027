On the desk Channel 02 · Week 1

The 12-bar blues, heard and counted

What we are learning

We are learning to hear and count the 12-bar blues form.

You can do it when

  • I can count a 12-bar chorus without help.
  • I can say where chords IV and V arrive.
In the room
01

The 12-bar form heard in three recordings, counted aloud, then diagrammed on the board.

02

How the genre is characterised: what makes a listener say “this is a blues” within two bars.

03

Count a full chorus without the teacher, and mark where the chord changes fall.

Worth keeping
—

A genre is recognisable because of features that can be named.
