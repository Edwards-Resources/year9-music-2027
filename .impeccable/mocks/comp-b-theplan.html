The 12-bar blues, heard and counted

What we are learning

We are learning to hear and count the 12-bar blues form.

You can do it when

  1. I can count a 12-bar chorus without help.
  2. I can say where chords IV and V arrive.
Worth keeping A genre is recognisable because of features that can be named.

In the room

01The 12-bar form heard in three recordings, counted aloud, then diagrammed on the board.
02What makes a listener say “this is a blues” within two bars.
03Count a full chorus without the teacher and mark where the changes fall.
