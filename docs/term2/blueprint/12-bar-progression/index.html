The blueprint · Blues to Jazz

12-bar progression

How it works

The frame every blues in this unit is built on, in numbers rather than letters, so it moves to whatever key you compose in.

  • Twelve bars, three systems of four: bar 1 is home (I), bar 5 moves away (IV), bar 9 pulls back (V).
  • The numbers do not change when the key does. Lesson 4 already asked you to prove that in three keys.
  • F is the class’s shared key, written in small type under each numeral. Your own piece can sit in any key that suits the players in your band.
