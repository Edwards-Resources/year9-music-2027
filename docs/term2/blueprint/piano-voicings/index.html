The blueprint · Blues to Jazz

Piano voicings

How it works

Four-note dominant 7th shapes for the chords the progression names: root, third, fifth and flattened seventh, stacked in that order.

  • Play the shape as a block on beat 1 of the bar it belongs to, then leave space. Comping is not strumming through the whole bar.
  • The same four numbers build the chord in any key: 1, 3, 5, ♭7. F7, B♭7 and C7 are written out because they are this progression’s three chords.
  • Guitar plays the same four notes on its own strings. Nobody in the rhythm section is exempt from knowing the shape.
