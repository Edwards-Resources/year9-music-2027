The blueprint · Blues to Jazz

Drum pattern

How it works

One bar of swing feel: the ride pattern that carries the time, the hi-hat closing on beats 2 and 4, and the snare backbeat sitting with it.

  • The ride is not four even quarter notes. It swings: long-short, long-short, twice a bar.
  • Hi-hat and snare land together, on beats 2 and 4. That is the backbeat, and it does not move.
  • Play the pattern from the notation once, then close the book and play it from memory. Lesson 15 is where that habit starts.
