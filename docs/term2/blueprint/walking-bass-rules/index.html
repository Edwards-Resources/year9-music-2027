Walking bass rules

The sheet

How it works

Two rules, applied at every chord change: land on the root, and approach the next root by step or by semitone.

  • Root on the change. The first note after a chord changes is always that chord’s root, in reach of the hand that is about to play it.
  • Approach by step or by semitone. The note right before a change leads into the next root from a note or a semitone away, never a leap.
  • Everything between the root and the approach note is your choice: a scale step, a chord tone, a passing note. The two rules bound it; they do not fill it in for you.
  • Everybody in the band can play a walking line, at pitch or in octaves. Lesson 12 is not only for whoever plays bass.
