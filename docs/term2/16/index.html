Lesson 16: Taking a risk in a solo

Lesson 16 of 30 · P16 · Jazz

  • Bring: Your instrument

Learning intention

We are learning to take a deliberate risk in a solo and carry it through.

Outcomes MU5-PER-01 MU5-PER-02

Success criteria

Tick them off as you go.

In classWhat we do

  1. In the roomWhat happens when a player leaves the scale, and how they get back. Both halves of that matter.
  2. With the bandTake one deliberate risk in your chorus. The room then says what it did to them as listeners.
  3. In the roomA wrong note held with confidence and a wrong note flinched at are two different events. That is the whole discussion.

Stage plot

You’ll need

  • Backing tracks
  • Recording device

Worth keeping

Risk taken deliberately is part of performing, not a failure of accuracy.
