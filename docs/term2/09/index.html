We are learning how music can influence and challenge the ideas people hold.

01

In the room

How this music travelled a long way past where it started, and what it did locally, nationally and internationally.

02

In the room

One recording examined closely: what it was for, who it was for, and what changed because of it. Knowing who was singing the work song, and where, changes what you hear in it.

03

On your own

Two dot points on context. Each one names the work, says what it did, and gives the evidence for saying so.

04

You can do it when

  • I can explain how a piece of music affected the people who heard it.
  • I can back the claim with evidence from the music or its history.
