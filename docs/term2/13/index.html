Comping, and the voicings supplied

Your instrument

We are learning to accompany a soloist rather than play underneath them.

01

In the room

The supplied piano and guitar voicings are taught and played, with the rhythmic placement that makes them comping rather than strumming.

02

With the band

Comping behind a soloist: listen for the gaps, and stay out of the register the melody is in.

03

On your own

Change one thing on purpose while you accompany: dynamics, texture, or how much you are playing.

04

You can do it when

  • I can play the supplied voicings.
  • I can comp behind a soloist without covering them.
