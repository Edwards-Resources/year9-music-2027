Lesson 26: Evaluating the elements in our cues

Lesson 26 of 30 · C19 · Popular music

  • Bring: Process log

Learning intention

We are learning to discuss the use of the elements in our own and others' compositions.

Outcomes MU5-COM-01 MU5-COM-02

Success criteria

Tick them off as you go.

In classWhat we do

  1. In the roomThree cues are analysed in depth by the class, one film and two game, with the composers sitting in the room.
  2. On your ownWrite the same analysis of your own work, at the same depth, using the term’s vocabulary precisely.
  3. In the roomVocabulary is corrected wherever it is loose. A word used nearly right is the thing that costs marks next year.

You’ll need

  • Scores and audio
  • Element checklist
  • Term vocabulary list

Worth keeping

Analysing your own work is the same skill as analysing anyone else’s.
