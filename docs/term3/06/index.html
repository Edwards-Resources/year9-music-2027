Lesson 6: Playing an Australian work

Lesson 6 of 30 · P14 · Music of Australia

  • Bring: Your instrument

Learning intention

We are learning to interpret repertoire from a particular genre and style.

Outcomes MU5-PER-01 MU5-PER-02

Success criteria

Tick them off as you go.

In classWhat we do

  1. In the roomThe class band plays Great Southern Land, interpreting it rather than reproducing it, with a part matched to what you can already play.
  2. With the bandName the conventions while you are playing them. What has to be right for this to sound like what it is?
  3. On your ownThe performance is recorded and played back against the original. Listen for the gap and say where it is.
