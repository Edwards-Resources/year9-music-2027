Lesson 9: Marking a dot point against the criteria

Lesson 9 of 30 · L8

  • Bring: Headphones and log

Learning intention

We are learning to judge a response the way a marker judges it.

Outcomes MU5-LIS-01 MU5-LIS-02

Success criteria

Tick them off as you go.

In classWhat we do

  1. In pairsReal responses, anonymised, marked against the AT3 criteria. Award the mark, then justify it to the person next to you.
  2. In the roomWhat a vague response costs, shown in marks rather than described. The marker’s view made visible.
  3. On your ownRemark your own Week 1 response. Understanding the criteria is faster than guessing what is wanted.

You’ll need

  • Anonymised responses
  • AT3 criteria
  • Marked exemplars

Worked exampleTwo takes

Question Describe the tone colour in the opening of the excerpt. 2 marks

Both of these are the length students actually write, and one of them is worth nothing. The difference is not effort and it is not vocabulary. It is whether the sentence could have been written by someone who never heard the excerpt.

The one that works

Bright and thin tone colour in the opening: one clean electric guitar with no distortion, high in its range, playing on its own for about eight bars before anything else comes in.

  1. Identify Name the quality, in words that belong to tone colour. Bright and thin. This is the first mark and it is the one most often thrown away, because “a nice sound” names no quality at all and could sit under any excerpt in the paper.
  2. Describe Add what is making that sound and where it is. One clean electric guitar, high in its range, alone for about eight bars. The test for the second mark is whether a marker could hear the opening from your words without playing the recording.
The one that doesn’t

The tone colour in the opening is very effective and creates a great atmosphere for the piece.

It reads like an answer, it is a whole sentence, and it earns nothing. Nothing in it could only have been written by someone who heard this excerpt: put any other opening under it and the sentence still stands. Effect without a named sound is the most common way a full page of writing earns no marks at all, and it is the thing to check for first when you mark somebody else’s response today.

Worth keeping

Understanding the criteria is faster than guessing what is wanted.
