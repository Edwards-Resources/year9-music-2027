The 12-bar blues, heard and counted

Unit
Blues to Jazz
Week
1
Content
L10
Repertoire
Jazz
Bring
Headphones

We are learning to hear and count the 12-bar blues form.

01

In the room

The 12-bar form heard in three recordings from the listening set, counted aloud together, then diagrammed on the board bar by bar.

02

In the room

How the genre is characterised: what makes a listener say “this is a blues” within two bars. Name the features rather than the feeling.

03

On your own

Count a full chorus without the teacher, and mark where the changes to chord IV and chord V fall.

04

You can do it when

  • I can count a 12-bar chorus without help.
  • I can say where chords IV and V arrive.
