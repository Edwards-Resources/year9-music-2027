Lesson 8: Singing the top line, playing the parts

Lesson 8 of 33 · P1 · Popular music

  • Bring: Your instrument

Learning intention

We are learning to sing and play with melodic and harmonic awareness.

Outcomes MU5-PER-01 MU5-PER-02

Success criteria

Tick them off as you go.

In classWhat we do

  1. In the roomThe whole class sings the melody with the harmony underneath it, listening for the places where the harmony pulls the tune somewhere you did not expect.
  2. In pairsTake a short section in two parts. Harmonic awareness is not something you can be told about; it is what happens when the other line is in your ear.
  3. On your ownThen play the melody on your own instrument, and keep it in tune with the part sounding against it.

Stage plot

You’ll need

  • Vocal warm-up card
  • Class repertoire in two parts

Worth keeping

Hearing your own part inside the harmony is what makes an ensemble work.
