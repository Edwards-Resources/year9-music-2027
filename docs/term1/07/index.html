Balance in the room

Unit
Like a Version
Week
3
Content
P7
Repertoire
Popular music
Bring
Your instrument

We are learning to balance an ensemble so the part that matters can be heard.

01

With the band

The class piece is played three times: once too loud, once with the melody buried, once balanced. You name the difference rather than being told it.

02

In the room

Amp and PA levels are then set as a decision. Where the knob was left last time is not a reason.

03

On your own

Rehearse with your section alone, then against one other section, and fix the balance by changing your own volume first.

04

You can do it when

  • I can hear when the important part is buried.
  • I can adjust my own volume to fix it.
