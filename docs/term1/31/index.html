The verse, and making it different

Unit
Like a Version
Week
11
Content
C3
Bring
Headphones and log

We are learning to develop contrasting material inside one song.

01

In the room

The verse is written against the chorus: lower, less movement, more words. The contrast is the entire point of it.

02

In flat.io

Assemble the full structure, verse and chorus in order, and listen to it end to end.

03

On your own

Compare your verse with your chorus and keep adjusting until the difference is audible to somebody who has not heard it before.

04

You can do it when

  • I can write a verse that is audibly different from my chorus.
  • I can explain how it is different.
